floating-ellipsis Advanced Playground GitHub

Advanced features.

Past the basics: elements sized by their content, icons and every script, lists, search snippets, whole numbers, tightening, text fields, quotations, Chinese and Japanese, and short forms. Drag any handle on this page.

01

Sized by content.

A chip or a flex item takes its width from its text, so once cut it stays narrow. With autoSize() it takes the width its full text would, and grows back when there is room. Narrow the box, then widen it again. And with lines: 'fit', text shows as many lines as its box is tall: drag the card's corner.

02

Icons, emoji and every script.

Images, icons and elements marked data-elide-unit show whole or not at all. Emoji, skin tones and the letters of any script are never split, wherever the cut falls.

03

Lists say how many are left.

With floating-ellipsis/list, items show whole or not at all, and a label counts the rest, joined as the list joins its last item. When not even the first fits, it's cut. Screen readers still read every item.

04

Snippets around a match.

Give a kept part context: up to two words either side of every match show first, a word at a time, after and then before, and the rest of the room goes to the start of the text. Type a word to search for.

05

Numbers stay whole.

A cut through $12,345.67 that shows $12,3… misreports it. With whole, matches show whole or not at all: a cut that would split one slides off it, hiding as much elsewhere. It's the rule words() follows, and the two combine.

06

Tighten before you cut.

Text a few pixels too long loses a whole ellipsis' worth of letters. With tighten, it's set a little tighter instead, word spaces most, just enough to end at the edge, and only cut once that isn't enough. With tightenCuts, a cut line is set tighter too, to show a few more letters.

07

Fields, cut until you edit.

floating-ellipsis/input shows a field's value cut while it isn't being edited. Click anywhere, even on the ellipsis: the whole value appears with the glyph you pressed still under the pointer, and the caret beside it.

08

Quotations, Chinese and Japanese.

Words left out of a quotation are marked […]: the brackets hug the dots, and the room goes either side, from a word space. Chinese and Japanese set their ellipsis in the middle of the line, six dots over two characters' cells. 'auto' does that for their text, whatever font the dots would come from, and takes exactly as many cells as it says, so the characters after it stay in their columns, even on a justified line.

09

Shorter before it's cut.

Wednesday can be Wed long before anything needs an ellipsis. With shorten, parts take their short forms a step at a time, as few as fit, the least important first; past the last, the text is cut. Copying, find in page and screen readers get the long forms, and so does the tooltip. dateForms writes a date down to 10/14/26 as its locale does, and data-elide-short gives markup its own.
