Balanced gaps

Dropping an ellipsis between two arbitrary letters leaves lopsided space, because every glyph carries its own side bearings. floating-ellipsis measures the ink and evens it out. By default it matches the gap between the dots themselves, so the ellipsis reads as part of the rhythm.

Keep parts, many ellipses

Pin what matters and let the rest give way. A kept part in the middle of a cut splits it into two ellipses, and you can ask for several ellipses outright. Kept parts are highlighted here.

Paths, hooks and tooltips

The instance tells you whether text is truncated, what each ellipsis hides, the line boxes and the exact ink of every dot. floating-ellipsis/tooltip builds on them: a headless tip that lines up letter for letter, or only answers the ellipsis. Rows are focusable too. Press on a full-path tip to select its text: it stays open while you hover it. Each list has a tooltip of its own, so after the first tip's delay, its neighbours show at once.
