Truncate text anywhere.

One line or many, the ellipsis wherever you ask, with the same visible gap on both sides and the line always ending at the edge. Drag any handle on this page.

$ npm i floating-ellipsis

React · Vue · Svelte · Web Components · Vanilla

01

Put the ellipsis anywhere.

From the start to the end, or anywhere between: a number from 0 to 1, or 'middle'.

02

Any number of lines and ellipses.

Clamp to a few lines and still cut in the middle of one, cut in several places at once, or draw the marks yourself.

Field notes

03

Suffixes are never cut.

A button, a badge or a time follows the text on its last line, and the text gives way around it. This button only shows when there's more to read.

04

Cut between words or path segments.

Cuts can fall between any two glyphs, or only where you say: at word boundaries, or at the separators of a path.

05

Tooltips only where text is cut.

floating-ellipsis/tooltip knows which rows are truncated. Hover or focus one that is: its tooltip grows out of the line, every glyph moving to make room for what was hidden.

    06

    Pin the parts that matter.

    Keep a file's extension, a search hit or a timestamp in sight. A kept part in the middle of a cut splits it in two.

    07

    Find in page finds it.

    None of these rows shows the word lantern. Press Ctrl F (⌘ F) and search for it: each match comes into sight, between two ellipses.

    08

    Customize: cut what’s shared.

    Siblings cut in the middle all look alike. Keep the segments no sibling has in their place, and cut the rest.
