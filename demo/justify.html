floating-ellipsis / justify

Where the room goes.

Truncating at whole words or path segments leaves a lot of room on the line. Each justify mode puts it somewhere: between the dots, around them, between the letters, or at the end. Each example shows three times: with the default repeat: false; with repeat: 0.75 (outlined), which fills that room with three quarters as many dots as fit there; and with repeat: 1 (dotted), as many as fit. Drag the width.
