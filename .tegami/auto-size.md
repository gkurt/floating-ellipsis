### `autoSize()` sizes an element by its full text, so it grows back

`autoSize()` from `floating-ellipsis/autosize` (or `<elide-text auto-size>`) is for an element whose width comes from its content, such as an inline-block, a float or a flex item. It takes the width its full text would, up to the room it has, instead of shrinking to the cut and staying there.
